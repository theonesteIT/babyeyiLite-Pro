# 08 — Extra Activities

Extra activities reserve timetable slots for non-lesson events.

## Steps

1. Open Extra Activities modal from Timetable page.
2. Create activity with class/day/time/term/year.
3. Validate slot capacity.
4. Save and sync to timetable.

## Notes

- Activities are stored separately then synced to `academic_timetables`.
- Validate after major generator runs to avoid hidden collisions.
